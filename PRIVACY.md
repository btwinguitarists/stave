# Stave Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 8, 2026*

Stave is a writing app. Your words belong to you, and the app is built so they never have to leave your hands.

## What Stave collects

Nothing.

Stave has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests with your content. The reference material it offers (Bible concordance, rhyming dictionary, word lists) is bundled inside the app and looked up entirely on your device.

## Where your writing lives

- **On Mac**, pages are plain Markdown files in your iCloud Drive `Stave` folder (or your Documents folder on Windows/Linux).
- **On iPad and iPhone**, pages are stored in the app's own Documents folder on your device. If you choose to connect a folder in iCloud Drive, pages are read from and written to that folder instead, using Apple's standard document access. That choice is yours, made through the system folder picker, and can only be made by you.

If your files are in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs them between your devices under [Apple's iCloud terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/). Stave itself transmits nothing.
